Privacy policy

This describes the privacy design of RankBuddy. It will be finalised before the first public release.

What RankBuddy stores

RankBuddy stores the HackerRank personal access token you enter in the macOS Keychain, and your app preferences locally on your Mac. It does not transmit them anywhere except to HackerRank.

Who RankBuddy talks to

RankBuddy communicates only with the HackerRank for Work API to fetch the data you ask it to display. There are no analytics services, advertising SDKs, or third-party telemetry endpoints.

What RankBuddy does not do

  • It does not collect usage analytics or crash data on a third-party server.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not require an account with the developer.

Your data, your control

Removing an account from RankBuddy deletes its stored token from the Keychain. Uninstalling the app removes its local preferences.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's issue tracker.

RankBuddy is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HackerRank.
